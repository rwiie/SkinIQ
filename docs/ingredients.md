# Ingredient standardization and ingredient-function mapping (proposal 3.5.2)

Generated by `python tools/make_ingredient_doc.py` from `data/knowledge/ingredient_aliases.csv` and `data/knowledge/ingredient_functions.csv`. Code: `skiniq/products.py`. Tests: `tests/test_ingredients.py`.

## Results on the product catalogue

- Products: 1,676 Korean skincare products after cleaning.
- Distinct ingredient names: 2,978 raw → 2,789 after standardization.
- Ingredient occurrences with a mapped function: 31,292 of 54,289 (58%); products with at least one mapped function: 1,662 of 1,676.
- Unmapped names are a long tail of plant extracts and minor ingredients, each used in few products.

## Table 3.5.2.1: Ingredient standardization

Names in brackets are checked both ways (`Water (Aqua)` → `Water`). Duplicate names within one product are removed.
